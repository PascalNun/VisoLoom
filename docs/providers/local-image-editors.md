# Local image editors for architectural rendering

Research reviewed 2026-11-20. VisoLoom uses image editing models to represent the complete Rhino view in the chosen style. The designer establishes the camera, geometry, materials and object placement in Rhino. The render aims to retain those decisions while translating the view into a competition sketch, photorealistic image, or another representation. The same view and representation choices apply when switching between compatible models.

## What to use for each representation

| Rhino workflow | First choice and comparisons | What to look for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Competition sketch or illustrated section | Start with FLUX.2 klein 4B on a textured or lightly shaded capture with clear edges. Compare LongCat Turbo and LLaDA Turbo for restyling, and FLUX 3 Image Klein with a complete caption. | The ink or wash can change extensively. Count wall openings, façade bays, stair treads and section cuts against the capture. Look for lines that invent joints or erase thin elements. |
| Photorealistic exterior or interior | Compare FLUX.2 klein 4B and FLUX 3 Image Klein first. Use the Rhino capture to establish light direction, glazing, planting positions and texture scale. Compare licensed FLUX.2 klein 9B or Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 on a workstation. | Check the silhouette, camera crop, mullions, material transitions and shadow direction before judging how photographic it looks. Inspect glazing and reflections for objects that moved or appeared. |
| Material or lighting study | Set the intended materials or lighting in Rhino and compare whole-view representations with FLUX.2 klein 4B, Qwen-Image-Edit-2511 and FLUX 3 Image Klein. | Compare whether the images retain the assigned finishes, texture scale, material boundaries and lighting direction throughout the view. |
| Full-frame geometry guidance | Compare a depth or edge-conditioned SDXL recipe with the ordinary reference-image workflow using the same Rhino camera and crop. | Check whether aligned geometry guidance helps retain the silhouette, openings and section lines across the frame. [ControlNet paper](https://arxiv.org/abs/2302.05543) |

These are priorities for comparing whole-view results, based on documented controls and our small FLUX.2 test. Public edit benchmarks such as [GEditBench v2](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.28547) are useful for style changes and instruction following. The [FireRed team's comparison](https://github.com/FireRedTeam/FireRed-Image-Edit/blob/main/docs/benchmark.md) includes Qwen 2511, LongCat and FLUX.2 dev on common tasks. Those results use different model revisions and evaluation setups from our proposed quantised Rhino workflows. Keep task-specific comparisons in the research, and use Settings for hardware fit, storage and access to weights.

## Commercial FLUX weights and offline use

- **FLUX.2 klein 4B** allows commercial inference under Apache 2.0. Once the compatible files and runner are installed, the local pipeline needs no BFL account or API call. **FLUX.2 klein 9B** and **FLUX.2 dev** have non-commercial default terms for operation of the model. Rights in outputs generated under those terms do not grant rights to run the model for paid client work. [BFL local-use FAQ](https://help.bfl.ai/articles/7108141705-can-i-run-or-fine-tune-flux-2-klein-locally) · [9B licence](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/FLUX.2-klein-9B/blob/main/LICENSE.md)
- **FLUX 3 Image Klein and Base** use the Kommunity licence. It permits qualifying users below US$5M in gross annualised revenue including affiliates to run the model to generate commercial outputs subject to its review or filtering and applicable disclosure conditions. It also has separate conditions for distributing weights and derivatives. The product page directs commercial product integrations to BFL licensing. A studio's own output rights and VisoLoom's right to distribute weights must each be assessed against their terms. [Kommunity licence](https://huggingface.co/black-forest-labs/flux-3-image-klein/blob/main/LICENSE.md) · [BFL FLUX 3 Image Klein](https://bfl.ai/models/flux-3-image-klein)
- **Paid self-hosting** gives rights for the licensed model, users and purpose. BFL describes Builder for internal operations and marketing, Platform for applications serving end users, and Professional for work on behalf of named clients. Confirm the exact checkpoint and intended architectural work with BFL before choosing a tier. The public self-hosted terms provide for usage reporting and may require tracking software. They do not establish a universal live check on each image or an unconditional offline entitlement for paid plans. Confirm how reporting, activation and renewal work in the actual agreement. The separate BFL image API sends the render to BFL and has its own access and billing. [Weight licensing and plan scopes](https://bfl.ai/open-weights) · [EU self-hosted terms](https://bfl.ai/legal/eu-self-hosted-commercial-license-terms) · [Self-hosted terms outside the EU](https://bfl.ai/legal/self-hosted-commercial-license-terms)

Keep licensed models visible with practical actions. Show **Download** for packages VisoLoom can offer directly. For gated or separately licensed weights, show **Get weights** at the publisher and **Use existing files…** after the customer obtains them. Confirm the model and supported recipe locally. Keep network activity during rendering independent from acquiring weights or satisfying any licence administration. Before offering a paid-weight integration, confirm its reporting and distribution rights explicitly. No generic FLUX subscription field belongs in Settings.

## Compare the Rhino outputs

Use three reproducible captures with a façade of repeated bays and glazing, a clipped stair and interior, and a concrete, timber and planting scene with deliberate lighting. Ask for the same whole-view representation and preserve the camera and frame. Record exact model revisions, precision, input sizes, caption or prompt, render time and peak memory with Rhino open. Compare a few outputs for drift and review failures at matching landmarks. Use architecture, material retention, representation and workstation cost as separate criteria.

Keep the first tests to one source PNG and a model-specific instruction. For FLUX 3 Image Klein, compare an authored full caption against a local caption expansion while retaining the image and intent. Explore aligned depth or edge guidance through compatible recipes where it helps preserve the whole view. Keep routine benchmarks and hardware evidence in the repo. Designers see model names, available sizes, hardware fit and applicable terms.
